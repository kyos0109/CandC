# Active discussion policy v1 acceptance

## Conclusion delivery and neutral discussion (2026-10-05)

- Apply the same delivery contract to technical, creative, philosophical and explicitly requested planning topics. No mandatory domain, scenario, stages, stance, verdict or public prose template. These fixtures check policy consistency, not live absence of model bias.
- Metadata-free proposals and confirmations without independent delivery review cannot produce a completed outcome. Saved answers are retained; correction is bounded to three turns per explicit execution and existing user/budget/storage gates.
- A peer can identify missing requested content, retain the review, return it to the author, and require a new proposal with reset peer confirmation. A positive assessment with nonempty gaps still requests revision.
- Complete answers may retain uncertainty, differing positions and nonblocking limitations. Partial delivery stays partial after all speakers agree; user stop and limit exhaustion do not imply completion.
- The conclusion page displays the final answer/basis/limitations first; stages collapse after an outcome, without contradicting the final status. Rejected drafts show gaps before continuation; reload and mobile retain the result.
- Old proposals load without rewriting; explicit continuation upgrades delivery and resets unchecked confirmations without rewriting messages. Existing summary/privacy/stop/storage checks remain required.

Define before implementation. Baseline: the existing v3 controller and prompt, copied to `.cache/verification/discussion-policy-baseline`. No live provider run is part of the deterministic evaluation.

Capability cases:

- Missing personal preferences: preserve a pending question and continue an independent analysis task, with and without a moderator.
- A repeated question key or equivalent normalized text updates one pending entry. Resolution requires a visible completed public user message; it is a model claim, not proof of understanding.
- Available research is explicit in the prompt. With research disabled, a research task cannot execute or imply a lookup; analysis remains possible.
- Premature judge pause/finish redirects to pending work, initial speaker coverage or a peer check, with bounded redirects. Muted seats are not forced to speak.
- Completed-task claims require eligible public sources. Private message IDs and other sessions' unseen messages cannot enter shared workflow state.
- Checkpoints persist atomically with public answers; malformed control saves the answer with a safe diagnostic but cannot change scheduling or publish workflow claims.
- Manual mode still stops at its contribution boundary. User stop, limits and uncertain storage never trigger extra synthesis calls.
- Explicit summary appears in the conclusion tab without declaring completion.
- Enable the policy or change research while idle: version-check, reset affected native sessions, preserve confirmed history, and never auto-start.
- Restart and uncertain append/sync/close recovery preserve committed questions/tasks/checkpoints and do not replay uncertain calls.

Regression cases: legacy v1/v2 and policy-less v3, independent sessions, same-provider seats, private inputs, stale controls, moderator stop authority, limits, topic notification, mixed journals and research boundaries.

Live comparison remains unrun: fixed model/effort, equal execution budgets, research on/off, moderator on/off, fixed source corpus, multiple topics and repeated trials. Human review measures unnecessary repeated questions, avoidable pauses, substantive progress, unsupported claims and cost. Fixture success proves controller behavior, not better live reasoning or a globally optimal design.

## Moderator authority and neutral-role revision (2026-10-05)

Added after the initial policy implementation in response to observed premature intervention in real journals. Deterministic acceptance covers:

- Ordinary facilitation is the default; speakers start first, no draft monitoring occurs, and one brief coordination call follows a full round or an independently confirmed outcome.
- Ordinary interrupt, mute, topic, pause and finish commands are rejected without cancelling speakers, changing the topic or creating a result. Stopping/silence alone is not confirmation.
- All speakers confirm the exact proposal before a participants' outcome. The facilitator receives only public proposal data and presents the result after confirmation; its routine metadata cannot replace speaker analysis.
- Explicit judge mode retains authorized forced intervention and a labeled unilateral ruling. The prompt advertises only the current role's allowed commands.
- Idle version-checked mode changes preserve history, retire sessions and do not auto-start. Old omitted-mode journals load without rewriting and select ordinary authority on explicit start.
- Prompts specify role/topic/evidence boundaries without mandatory decision frameworks or fixed answers. Speaker roles permit disagreement, agreement and revision; facilitator capability limits do not prohibit authorized speaker research.
- Real control keys such as `purchase_context` decode strictly; invalid controls retain diagnostics rather than triggering automatic replay.
- Browser creation defaults the judge option off; settings toggles survive reload, do not launch providers and fit the mobile viewport.

No live provider comparison was added. Tests establish permission, scheduling, persistence and prompt content; they cannot guarantee model brevity, spontaneous discussion quality or optimality across topics.
